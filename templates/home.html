Don't wait for the accident. Detect the warning signs.

Two safety inputs. One SIF intelligence workflow.

Our prototype brings together manual UA / UC / Near-Miss reports and CCTV-detected unsafe events. Both paths feed a common safety intelligence layer that identifies potential Serious Injury & Fatality (SIF) precursors, assesses risk, recommends action and stores the result for dashboard analysis.
